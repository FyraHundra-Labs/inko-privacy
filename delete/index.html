Delete Your Inko Account

You can delete your account directly in the Inko app. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

Steps

  1. Open the Inko app
  2. Tap the settings icon (top-right corner)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when prompted

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile (phone number, name, username)
  • All check-in history
  • Your connections (circle)
  • Push notification tokens

What is not deleted

  • Other users' connections that referenced your phone number (they will see you as inactive)
  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google (managed by the respective store)

Account deletion is immediate and irreversible.

If you cannot access the app, contact us at support@fyrahundra.com and we will delete your account within 48 hours.
